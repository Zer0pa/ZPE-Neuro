# Code Of Conduct

This repo follows an evidence-first contributor standard.

## Our Pledge

Participation in ZPE-Neuro should be harassment-free and technically honest. Negative findings, inconclusive results, and claim corrections are valid contributions here.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat evidence as the authority surface
- Keep unresolved findings visible instead of narrating them away
- Separate disagreement from disrespect
- Document scope limits and reproduction details clearly

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Suppressing failing or inconclusive evidence
- Inflating claims beyond what the proof surface supports
- Publishing private information without consent

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to `architects@zer0pa.ai`.

## Project-Specific Norm

Evidence discipline is a community rule here. Unsupported claims and attempts to hide contradictory artifacts are conduct problems, not style differences.
